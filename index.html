    Marketing Intelligence

    Track ad performance, website traffic, landing page visibility, and lead sources

    How to read this page — click to expand

    Business Scorecard

    Auto-calculated weekly snapshot of the whole business. You don't enter anything here — it pulls from your CRM (leads), Estimating (estimates, jobs won/lost, revenue sold), and Invoicing (revenue collected, cash on hand).

    Leads = clients added in CRM that week

    Estimates = estimates created that week

    Jobs Won = estimates marked "Approved" that week

    Jobs Lost = estimates marked "Declined" that week

    Close Rate = Jobs Won ÷ (Jobs Won + Jobs Lost)

    Revenue Sold = dollar value of approved estimates that week

    Revenue Collected = dollar value of invoices marked "Paid" that week

    Cash on Hand = total of all paid invoices (cumulative)

    Avg Job Value = Revenue Sold ÷ Jobs Won

    Jobs Won Trend

    Line chart of jobs won (approved estimates) over time. Use the View toggle to switch between weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly to see whether you're growing.

    Decline Reasons

    Shows why you're losing jobs. When you mark an estimate "Declined," you pick a reason. This chart groups all decline reasons for the selected period so you can spot patterns.

    Ad KPI Entry (Campaign Tracker)

    Manual entry — one row per ad campaign per week. Enter what you spent and what you got back.

    Spend = how much you spent on the ad

    Revenue Closed = revenue from jobs that ad produced

    CPL (Cost Per Lead) = Spend ÷ Leads (auto-calculated)

    ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) = Revenue Closed ÷ Spend (auto-calculated, shown as a ratio like "40x" meaning $40 returned for every $1 spent)

    ROAS guide: 5x+ = great, 2-5x = okay, 1-2x = barely profitable, below 1x = losing money

    Web Tracking

    Shows website visits and tracked leads from the public website tracker. Counts start when the tracker is installed on the live site.

    Lead Source Mix

    Shows where your leads came from (Google, referral, repeat, etc.) — pulled automatically from the CRM's lead source field.
